Kerala Honeymoon Package

A delightful 4-day romantic getaway focusing on the two most iconic highlights of Kerala: the cool hills of Munnar and the tranquil backwaters of Alleppey.

Overview

Designed for couples with tight schedules who want an unforgettable romantic trip. Explore Munnar's misty hills, lush tea gardens, cascading waterfalls, and Alleppey's serene backwaters with a private houseboat stay.

This package combines natural beauty, relaxation, and memorable experiences for the perfect honeymoon.
