Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Crazy Tours and Travels, we believe your personal information belongs to you. We collect only what is strictly necessary to organize, quote, and fulfill your journey, and we never sell or distribute your data to third-party marketing brokers.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry form or book a journey with Crazy Tours, we collect:

  • Contact details (name, email address, phone number).
  • Travel preferences (dates, party size, pace, destination choices).
  • Operational travel details required for bookings (dietary requirements, emergency contacts, passport details for national park permits).

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Design custom routes and calculate guaranteed fixed pricing.
  • Coordinate reservations with our locally based hotels, transport providers, and wilderness guides.
  • Send our monthly newsletter if you have explicitly chosen to subscribe (you can unsubscribe at any time with one click).

4. Data Storage and Security

All communications and enquiry records are transmitted using encrypted SSL protocols. We maintain secure technical standards to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Contact Our Privacy Team

If you have questions regarding your personal information, or wish to request data deletion, please contact our team at crazytraveller11@gmail.com.
