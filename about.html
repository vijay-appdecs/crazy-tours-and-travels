About Crazy Tours and Travels

Small on purpose. Local by design.

We founded Crazy Tours and Travels in 2015 with a simple belief: the best travel experiences happen in groups small enough to fit around one dinner table, led by people who call the destination home.

Our Philosophy

Why we cap our groups at 12 travelers

A group of 30 is a crowd. It requires chartered mega-buses, standardized buffet dining rooms, and audio headsets so people can hear a guide three rows back.

A group of 12 is a dinner party. It fits into family-owned trattorias, catches local island ferries, walks mountain trails in silence, and slips into neighborhood wine cellars without disrupting village life.

Scouted in Person

Every route is walked by our team before it goes on sale

We do not license third-party white-label packages or copy itineraries from internet forums. Before a Wayfare route is published, our own trip designers spend weeks walking the footpaths, testing luggage transfers, and staying in every bed.

If a hotel loses its charm or a trail gets overrun by construction, we change the route. That independence is why over 60% of our travelers book their second and third trips with us.
